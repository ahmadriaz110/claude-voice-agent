Read this aloud in your normal speaking voice, at your normal pace, for about three minutes. Do not perform it. Pause where you would pause.

My name is your name here and I work in your role at your company. Most of my day is spent on the phone, in meetings, and writing to people who need something from me. I use this assistant to keep track of what comes in while I am busy, and to get work started without stopping what I am doing.

Please count slowly from one to twenty. Then say the days of the week, and the months of the year.

Now describe the room you are in. What is on the desk, what you can see out of the window, what the weather is doing today. Talk about what you had for breakfast and what you plan to do this evening.

Read a few sentences from any book or article near you. Then say a few sentences in any other language you speak at home, the way you would say them to family.

Finally, say the wake phrase ten times, with a short gap between each: hey Claude. hey Claude. hey Claude.
